# What to ask Carmen, and what to send her

Everything below is drafted for Chase to send. Gable talks to Carmen; it does
not ask her for verdicts on its own work.

## The one question that unblocks the most

Send her **one flyer** — the `Just Listed — Compact Stats Bar` output, which is
verified clean: correct address, correct price, beds/baths/square footage found
by research rather than typed, her agent's real headshot and contact details, no
placeholder text, no stray content.

Ask exactly this:

> Would you post this as it is, or what would you change first?

Not "is this good?" — that invites politeness. "Would you post it" has a yes or
no behind it, and "what would you change first" gets the single most important
defect rather than a list she has to compile.

**Why this one question matters more than any amount of further engineering.**
Every remaining judgement about layout quality is currently an inference about
her standard. Tonight established, repeatedly, that inference loses to evidence.
Her answer either confirms the bar or redirects the work; without it the next
round of changes is aimed at a guess.

## What she does not need to look at

The layout complaints. Every rendered flyer was compared against the blank
template it came from: **no text collision, and no text pushed off the slide,
was introduced by Gable.** Where a flyer overlaps, its design already did.

So a layout objection from her is worth having as a design note, not as a bug
report against the pipeline.
